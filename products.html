Product range

Six specifications, one titanium vacuum platform.

Every Zebscan product is built on the same titanium-spacer vacuum core — two tempered panes, a 0.3 mm vacuum gap, and titanium spacers, edge seal and absorption strip in place of the aluminium competing products still use. What changes between products is the coating: clarity, solar control, acoustic performance or fire resistance.
