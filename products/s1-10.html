All-round performance

S1.10

A colour-neutral workhorse, down to 6.7 mm for the narrowest historic frames.

S1.10 is the product most projects end up specifying. A single neutral coating keeps colour rendition honest — no green or blue cast through the pane — while U=0.40 puts it well inside triple-glazing territory at a fraction of the thickness.

At its slimmest, 6.7 mm total build, it is thin enough to sit inside original wooden or steel sashes that were never designed to take insulated glass at all. That single fact is usually what makes a retrofit possible instead of a full frame replacement.
